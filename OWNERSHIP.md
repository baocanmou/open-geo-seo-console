## Exclusions

This repository does not license or publish:

- production credentials, customer or site inventories, account data, or

- private thresholds, customer-specific strategies, production orchestration,
  server topology, or operational records;
- the proprietary BCM GEO production platform as a whole;
- third-party packages, except under the permissions provided by their own

- trademarks, logos, service identity, or rights in user-provided data.

## Contributions

Until a reviewed contribution agreement and acceptance process are in place,
the project accepts issues, reproducible test cases, and design discussion but
does not merge unsolicited external source code. This prevents an ambiguous
chain of title. See `CONTRIBUTING.md`.

## Evidence of origin

`PROVENANCE.md` records independent implementation, fixed conceptual research
references, and the release checks used to prevent accidental third-party or
private material from entering the repository. `ORIGIN.json` provides the same
boundary in machine-readable form.
